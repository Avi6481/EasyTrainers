# EasyTrainer

A trainer menu for Cyberpunk 2077, built on Cyber Engine Tweaks. Works with keyboard, mouse and controller.

## What's in it

- Self - god mode, wanted level, NoClip, movement and more
- Modifiers - RAM, cooldowns, resistances, ability durations
- Development - attributes, perks, skills and levels
- Teleports - map pins, fast travel points, your waypoint and saved spots
- Vehicles - spawn any car, garage, tuning, paint, lights, speedometer
- Weapons - ammo, tuning, special rounds, dual wield support
- Items - add any item, quick adds, crafting recipes
- Game Facts, Time and Weather
- World Editor - free camera, pick up and move anything, spawn props and vehicles, attach things together
- Settings - themes, layout, navigation, hotkeys and language

Nothing the trainer does is written into your saves. Turn an option off and the game's own values come back.

## Requirements

- Cyber Engine Tweaks
- RED4ext
- redscript
- EasyInputHandler and EasyTrainerUtility, both included in the Nexus download

## Install

Download it from Nexus and drop the `bin`, `red4ext` and `r6` folders into your Cyberpunk 2077 folder.

## Open the menu

F4 on keyboard, RB + D-pad Right on controller. You can change it in Settings.

## Native parts

The two DLLs are open source and built on GitHub:

- EasyInputHandler - https://github.com/Avi6481/EasyInputHandler
- EasyTrainerUtility - https://github.com/Avi6481/EasyTrainerUtility
